# beans-batch-3 notes (researched 2026-10-17)

## Skipped shops (café, no retail bean line)
- **Arlington Five** (5 Arlington Ave) — café/arts space, not a roaster. Brews LULO Coffee (a nano-roastery that roasts on-site weekly and sells recyclable tins via its own website), plus Happy Goat and The Artery. No retail beans sold under the Arlington Five name. Future batch candidate: LULO Coffee as a roaster-based entry.
- **Planet Coffee** (24a York St) — café in the ByWard Market serving Fluid Coffee Roasters (solar-powered roaster) beans. No verifiable retail-bean listing on Planet Coffee's own site (a third-party review notes only a couple of bags sold in-store). Future batch candidate: Fluid Coffee Roasters.
- **Bar Robo** (170 Queen St) — café/cocktail bar and live-music venue inside the Queen St Fare food hall. Not a roaster; no retail beans found anywhere.

## Data caveats
- **Bridgehead compostable pods** (Centro House / Bytown Boom pods) were excluded — bagged retail range only (6 beans × 4 locations = 24 entries).
- **Bridgehead "Honduras"** — site product title is "APROCOMSA - Honduras"; product sold as "Honduras"; roast Medium confirmed by the site's own copy ("this medium roast from Marcala").
- **Bridgehead "Steady Hands Decaf"** — roast level not stated in text; read as Medium off the printed roast scale on the bag photo.
- **Little Victories "Sunshine"** — house pillar whose lot rotates; currently Guatemala CCDA, so seeded as origin Guatemala. (LVC stock rotates frequently.)
- **Little Victories limited jars** (4 entries) are 100g jars, not 340g bags — noted in the bean names.
- **Little Victories "Roaster's Choice"** — skipped; it's whatever the roasters pick that week, not a stable product. Subscriptions duplicating the same coffees were also skipped.
- **Equator "Hang Loose Half-Caf"** — half-caffeine, so `decaf: false`; empty tasting notes (site lists none).
- **Equator "Polaris Espresso"** — site lists no tasting notes; roast Medium per site tags.
- **Equator legacy blends** (Sweet Justice, Freakin' Good, Outlier, Nth Degree) are mentioned only in the Sampler Bundle description; they have no live retail product pages and were omitted. The site's current retail range is the 8 included.
- **Equator "Colombia Cauca"** — described as "light-medium"; seeded as Light.
- Image URLs were visually verified as real product photos for one Bridgehead bag (Honduras, Steady Hands), three LVC assets (Sunshine bag, Bright Eyed bag, limited jar) and one Equator bag (Guatemala Huehuetenango); the remaining URLs come from the same stores' official product feeds and follow the same bag-render naming pattern.
