## Data caveats
- **imageUrl omitted everywhere.** Sam James's product pages (samjamescoffeebar.com) exposed full tasting notes and descriptions but no image URLs through text fetch, so all 9 Sam James beans ship without photos. Jimmy's Coffee's site (jimmyscoffee.ca) is minimal and carries no bean details or photos at all — bag names verified via a third-party café review (torontocoffeedate.ca). Dineen's online shop (dineencoffee.shop) could not be fetched (rate-limited), so the Temperance Roast entry has no photo. Neo's Blend bag photo exists only on its SkipTheDishes menu listing, not on Neo's own site, so it was omitted per the no-guessing rule. If photos are needed later, a live-browser pass over samjamescoffeebar.com product pages is the fastest way to capture them.
- **Jimmy's Coffee beans (Dean, Hendrix, Carter, Hoffa)** — the four blend names are verified from the torontocoffeedate.ca review of Jimmy's Roncesvalles ("The different blends of coffee are all named after a different famous Jimmy ('Dean', 'Hendrix', 'Carter', 'Hoffa')") plus confirmation that bags of beans are sold in-café. Only "Hoffa" has verified tasting notes (sweet, caramel, cocoa — from a restaurantji.com review of the 735 Queen St W location describing "the bean of the day"). The other three have empty tastingNotes. Roast level is a judgment call (Medium); origins are unknown (Blend). The full bag names may be e.g. "Jimmy Dean" but only the single-word blend names could be verified, so those were used verbatim.
- **Dineen Coffee Co. — Temperance Roast only.** One bean verified: "Temperance Roast — our signature roast named after our flagship store is a single origin Brazilian bean with tasting notes of hazelnut, caramel and chocolate" (Ritual ordering menu for Dineen Commerce Court). Dineen's full online-shop bean range could not be verified because the shop page fetch was rate-limited; there may be additional roasts (espresso/decaf) worth a later pass.
- **Neo Coffee Bar — Neo Blend only.** Neo does not roast its own coffee — the site sources from "Toronto based roasters" plus monthly guest producers — so the "roaster" field is set to "Neo Coffee Bar" as the brand selling the bag; the actual roast partner is unverified. One retail bag found: "Neo Blend — 100% Brazil, medium roast, whole beans" ($18.95 on the Neo King x Frederick SkipTheDishes menu; also appears in the "Start your Day" Coffee Set as "Neo blend 227g"). No tasting notes published.
- **Sam James roast level:** all 9 beans set to Medium per the site's own statement on the beans collection page: "Medium roasted coffee, in Toronto, since 2009."
- **Buzz Lite** is a half-caffeinated blend (decaf: false), not a true decaf — flagged here so the app doesn't show it as caffeine-free. "Decaf: Colombia" is the only true decaf (decaf: true).
