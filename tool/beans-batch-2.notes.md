# Beans Batch 2 — notes

Research date: 2026-10-17. Source: roasters' own websites via browser (text fetch + image footnotes).

## Skipped shops

- **Drip House** (340B Parkdale Ave, Ottawa) — café/brunch spot (driphousecoffee.ca). No retail beans sold, no bean shop page. Skipped as a café that does not sell retail beans.
- **Ottawa Bike Cafe** (79 Sparks St, Ottawa) — café/bike shop; brews Fluid Solar Roasted Coffee (not its own roast) and sells no retail beans of its own. Skipped as a café that does not sell retail beans.
- **Café Cloudforest** (339 Richmond Rd, Ottawa) — excluded from this batch; covered by a sibling batch under the identical name.

## Morning Owl caveats (both "Morning Owl" and "Morning Owl Coffeehouse" name sets)

- Morning Owl roasts in-house in small batches (verified on morningowl.ca), but their site lists **no individual bean product pages** — only an in-store trio (Medium / Dark / Espresso, sold out online) and a wholesale 5 lb bag.
- The 3 beans per name set ("Dark Roast", "Medium Roast", "Espresso Roast") come from the site's own "3 Bags Combination - Dark, Medium, and Espresso" product; **imageUrl omitted** (no verifiable bag photos); tastingNotes empty (none published).
- `origin: "Blend"` is a placeholder — the site publishes no origin for these generic roast-level bags. The app should treat it as "unknown blend".
- Descriptions paraphrased from the Morning Owl homepage ("roasted fresh in-house and in small batches").
- Note: newer Morning Owl franchise locations (Kanata+) reportedly use Equator Coffee Roasters beans for brewed coffee (apt613), so in-cup =/= beans sold; the bags sold are Morning Owl's own roast per their site.

## Happy Goat caveats

- **Roaster's Choice - 340g** excluded: gift-style product with no fixed bean/origin ("let our roasters pick for you").
- **Ethiopia Guji** appeared on the homepage's featured strip but is **not in the full /collections/all catalog** (29 products) and has no active product page — treated as discontinued and skipped.
- Roast badges on collection cards precede the product they describe (verified against product pages).
- Brazil Mogiana Espresso and Tesseract Espresso are espresso roasts the site describes as medium-to-medium-dark; both recorded as `roastLevel: "Medium"` per the Light/Medium/Dark schema.
- The "G" is a medium+dark house blend of Brazil and Guatemala; recorded as Medium (its own product card alt text says "Multi Region Medium Roast Coffee") and `origin: "Blend"`. Tesseract Espresso (Ethiopia + Brazil) likewise `origin: "Blend"`.
- All Happy Goat imageUrl values are product bag photos from the product pages' image footnotes on happygoatcoffee.com (not guessed).
- `local: true` — Happy Goat roasts in Ottawa (Hintonburg/Laurel); Morning Owl roasts in Ottawa.
